### About me

For 25+ years I've helped people by revealing the simplicity in the complex. Today that means AI and emerging technology — understanding it, building with it, and teaching others to use it well.

- 🤖 **AI & emerging tech** — building agentic, AI-assisted systems on Amazon Bedrock and Claude, and teaching teams how to adopt them responsibly.
- 🎓 **Cloud & AI training** — AWS certification and exam-prep workshops for enterprise, consulting and public-sector teams, from AI Practitioner and Machine Learning to Security Specialty.
- ✍️ **Courseware & exam authoring** — co-authoring part of Udacity's *AWS Solutions Architect Associate* Nanodegree; practice-exam item writing and technical review for *GitHub Foundations (GH-900)*, *AWS AI Practitioner* and *AWS Security Specialty*.
- 🏫 **Teaching** — IT at Pine Technical & Community College.

### What I'm building

Most of my code lives in private repositories, so here's the shape of it:

- **[Prifio](https://learnflourish.com/prifio/)** — *where learning products grow.* A human-led framework and platform for the whole learning lifecycle, from analysing the need through design, delivery and assessment to evidence that informs the next cycle. It brings standards, workflows and quality gates to courseware development, and keeps courseware, hands-on labs, assessments and results in one place.
- **A farm-management platform for [Wild Haven Farm LLC](https://sunrisewildhaven.com)** (Minnesota) — livestock, apiary and crop records plus organic-certification reporting, on a serverless AWS stack (Lambda, DynamoDB single-table design, CDK, Astro + React). It includes **voice-captured hive inspections** (Amazon Transcribe → Amazon Bedrock extraction → human review) and AI-assisted lab-report entry.
- **AI-assisted engineering** — agentic workflows with Claude Code and Kiro, with guardrails (hooks, policy checks, review gates) so AI moves fast without breaking governance.
- **Governed AWS foundations** — multi-account landing zones with Control Tower, organisation-wide backup and security baselines, and Well-Architected reviews baked into CI.
